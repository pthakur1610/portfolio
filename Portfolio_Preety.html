The Product Vision

Agentic AI compliance, packaged as a product — not a policy PDF

Every enterprise shipping an agent that recommends, decides or pays on a customer's behalf needs the same four-layer governance spine. Today most teams build it once, bespoke, per project. The product opportunity is to make that spine configurable — so the same core ships into insurance, banking, healthcare or telecom, and adapts to whichever regulator is in the room.

Product thesis

"Governance-as-configuration," localized by country, not rebuilt by country.

The four control layers — policy configuration, guardrail checks, human-in-the-loop routing, and audit & traceability — stay constant. What changes per market is the rule set sitting inside them: disclosure thresholds, consent language, escalation SLAs and data-residency rules. A governance product should let a compliance lead configure that difference without a single line of code changing — the same instinct behind her existing policy-configuration work, applied at product scale.

EUROPEAN UNION

EU AI Act

Risk-tiered obligations for high-risk AI systems, transparency duties and human-oversight requirements — maps directly onto the human-in-the-loop layer.

UNITED STATES

NIST AI RMF + state rules

A voluntary risk-management framework plus a growing patchwork of state-level AI and insurance-conduct rules — configuration has to flex state by state.

INDIA

DPDP Act · RBI / IRDAI guidance

Data-protection consent rules plus sector regulators for banking and insurance — the exact regulatory pair her BFSI experience already sits inside.

SINGAPORE

MAS FEAT / AI governance

Fairness, ethics, accountability and transparency principles for financial-sector AI — a natural template for the guardrail-check layer.

UNITED KINGDOM

ICO & FCA expectations

Outcomes-based conduct regulation for financial services — reinforces why "disclosure until source figure confirmed" has to be a configurable rule, not a hardcoded one.

GLOBAL SPINE

ISO/IEC 42001 · GARP RAI™

The management-system standard and risk curriculum she's already mapped her four-layer model against — the constant core every local rule set plugs into.
